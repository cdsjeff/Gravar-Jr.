Gravar Jr. Help

v1.38 — Visible Frame Clip

Video loading

Open the folder menu and choose Local file, YouTube, or Panopto. Local files provide the most complete browser control, including visible frame capture and accurate timing. YouTube and institutional embeds may restrict capture or time control depending on browser and source permissions.

Transport and picture control

Use the side controls for ±5 seconds, ±1 second, and Play/Pause. Command-click/Ctrl-click the ±1 second arrows for ±0.1 second. Hold the ±1 second arrows to step at about 7 frames per second.

Picture zoom: Command-Option drag up/down, pinch/trackpad pinch, or the video +/− buttons enlarge or shrink only the image inside the original visible video picture. The black surrounding pane and the original visible picture boundary stay fixed. When zoomed, plain click-drag repositions the enlarged picture within that fixed picture boundary.

Scrubbing: Command-Option drag left/right on the video picture to scrub forward/backward.

Transcript lines

Use Start and End to time the selected transcript line. Click the line number/check area to seek to that line’s Start. Double-click/tap the time areas to show editable timecodes. Double-click/tap or Command-click/Ctrl-click a transcript line to play from its Start to End.

Notes and exports

Use ▣ for line-specific notes. Use ◇ Field Note for the project-wide rich-text field note. Field Note supports formatting, tables, and frame clips. CSV and HTML exports include transcript timing, line notes, and field notes. HTML export includes available frame-clip images and timing information. Fr Clip captures the currently visible cropped portion of the picture when the video is zoomed/panned, not the full source frame.
